Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the website sergiopereez.github.io/makingyoulearn and the “Making You Learn uploader” app, an internal tool that uploads videos produced by Making You Learn to the Making You Learn YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with the account owner's permission (Google OAuth). It uses that access only to upload our own videos and to set their title, description, privacy status and thumbnail, and to read basic information about our own channel. It does not read, store or share data from any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

The only data stored is the OAuth refresh token for our own channel, kept in private, access-controlled storage. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Making You Learn Publisher (TikTok)

Making You Learn Publisher (sergiopereez.github.io/makingyoulearn/publisher/) lets a creator sign in with TikTok and post a video they choose to their own TikTok account. With the creator's permission (TikTok Login Kit) it uses the scopes user.info.basic, to show the creator's avatar and nickname, and video.publish, to post the video with the caption, privacy and interaction settings the creator selects.

The video file is sent straight to TikTok through our server and is not kept. The TikTok access token lives only in the browser tab (session storage) and is erased when the tab is closed or the creator picks "Switch account"; our server does not store tokens, videos or profile data. We do not sell or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy. Creators can remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal data.
