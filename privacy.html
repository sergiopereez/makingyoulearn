Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the website sergiopereez.github.io/makingyoulearn and the “Making You Learn uploader” app, an internal tool that uploads videos produced by Making You Learn to the Making You Learn YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with the account owner's permission (Google OAuth). It uses that access only to upload our own videos and to set their title, description, privacy status and thumbnail, and to read basic information about our own channel. It does not read, store or share data from any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

The only data stored is the OAuth refresh token for our own channel, kept in private, access-controlled storage. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal data.
